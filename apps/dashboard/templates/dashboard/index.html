What to do if a will is disputed or ignored

If you believe a will is fake, unfair, or wasn't signed properly, you can lodge a caveat at court before probate is granted. This stops the executor from acting until a judge hears your objection. If someone dies without a valid will, the estate is divided by a fixed legal formula instead of family agreement, which is why disputes and property grabbing from widows and children are common. Executors must file an inventory and account for the estate to the court and the Administrator General, they cannot simply ignore the will. For help, contact the Administrator General's office in Kampala or Justice Centres Uganda's free legal aid line.

Source: Succession Act, Cap 162, and the Succession (Amendment) Act 2022
